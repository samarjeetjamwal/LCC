C Order-to-Cash — Export Sale, Proforma to Collection

In distributor/export markets, I've owned the full O2C cycle — not just the collections tail end — which is what lets a debt control function actually prevent disputes upstream instead of just chasing them downstream.

1
Proforma Invoice

Issued/verified proforma against agreed pricing, credit terms and distributor credit limit before order release.

2
Order & Credit Check

Ran the order against the customer's live credit-risk score; cleared or held based on exposure.

3
Dispatch Documentation

Coordinated shipment/logistics docs so invoicing matched what was actually dispatched — no leakage from mismatched paperwork.

4
Commercial Invoice

Raised the final invoice, applying agreed terms, deductions and statutory (VAT) treatment accurately.

5
Follow-up & Collection

Tracked promise-to-pay against due date; escalated per the aging bucket the account fell into.

6
Cash Application & Ledger Close

Applied cash, reconciled the customer account, and closed the ledger clean — ready for the next credit cycle.
